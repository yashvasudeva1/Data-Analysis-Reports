Battery Energy Storage (BESS) & Pumped Storage Mandate
Storage Architecture • Ramp Management
With solar generation crossing 149.2 TWh and midday solar clipping exceeding 55 GW, the grid faces an immediate 28,000 MW evening ramp deficit between 17:00 and 20:30. India must accelerate the deployment of co-located 4-hour Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and Pumped Storage Plants (PSP) at renewable hubs in Rajasthan and Gujarat.

Green Energy Corridors & Inter-Regional Transmission De-Bottlenecking
Transmission Infrastructure • Spatial Balancing
Our spatial analysis reveals that the Western Grid (484.9 TWh) and Southern Grid (410.8 TWh) generate significant renewable surpluses, while the Northern Grid (464.2 TWh) suffers acute pre-monsoon deficits during May-June heatwaves. Fast-tracking High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) interstate links from Western solar parks to Northern consumption hubs will prevent regional grid frequency excursions.

Western-to-Northern Flow +18,000 MW Wheeling

Dynamic Time-of-Day (ToD) Tariff Restructuring
Demand-Side Management • Market Economics
Commercial and industrial tariffs currently offer insufficient incentives to shift load into daylight hours. State electricity regulators (SERCs) must introduce mandatory Time-of-Day tariffs featuring a 25% tariff discount during solar hours (10:00–15:00) and a 30% surcharge during evening peak hours (18:00–22:00). This will incentivize commercial cooling pre-chilling and scheduled EV fleet charging during peak solar generation.
Daytime Solar Discount 25% Rebate (10:00-15:00)
Peak Demand Flattening 8,500 MW Shift
EV Fleet Day Alignment 70% Daytime Charging

Coal Baseload Flexibilization & 40% Minimum Technical Load (MTL)
Generation Fleet Operations • Inertia Preservation
Coal generation continues to supply 1,173 TWh (71.5%) of electricity and provides critical spinning inertia. However, inflexible plants forced to run above 55% capacity during midday risk forced outages. Thermal units must be retrofitted with sliding pressure control and burner modifications to operate down to 40% Minimum Technical Load without oil support, enabling flexible cycling under the National Merit Order Despatch framework.
Target Minimum Technical Load 40% MTL (down from 55%)
Ramping Capability 3.0% / Minute Ramp Rate
Avoided Plant Shutdowns 14,000 MW Baseload

Agricultural Feeder Solarization (PM-KUSUM Component-C)
Rural Grid Reliability • Discom Health
In high-irrigation agrarian states like Uttar Pradesh (145.4 TWh) and Punjab, agricultural water pumping creates sharp nocturnal load spikes that force expensive thermal dispatch. Solarizing dedicated agricultural feeders transitions farm pumping to daytime solar hours, eliminating discom subsidies on night supply and relieving distribution transformers of nocturnal overloads.
Target Ag Feeder Solarization 3.5 Million Pumps
Night Load Removal 11,200 MW Off-Peak Shift
Discom Subsidy Relief ₹8,200 Cr Annually
