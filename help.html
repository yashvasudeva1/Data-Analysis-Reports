Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload a new dataset?
Navigate to the Datasets page from the sidebar menu. You can drag and drop your CSV, JSON, or Excel files into the upload zone, or click it to browse your computer.
What file formats are supported?
Analysis Hub supports parsing for .csv, .json, .xlsx, and .xls files. Raw dataset sources from portals like Kaggle and Data.gov.in are supported.
How long does report processing take?
Standard datasets (under 10MB) typically process within 30 seconds. Larger datasets or complex predictive models (like the Crime Analysis and CPI Forecasts) may take a few minutes. You can monitor progress on the Dashboard.
Can I export my charts and reports?
Yes, when viewing any generated report, click the "Export Report" button in the top right corner to print or save the document as a PDF.
